Research

Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of probability, statistical inference, and machine learning. I am interested in developing mathematical and computational methods for learning stochastic systems, making decisions under uncertainty, and recovering structure from noisy data.

SDE Inference RL and Bandits GMMs and Video Denoising

Inference for Stochastic Differential Equations

I am interested in learning stochastic dynamics from observed data, particularly when a system involves multiple time scales or unobserved components. My research combines stochastic differential equations (SDEs), statistical inference, and flexible probabilistic models to learn effective dynamics and quantify uncertainty.

In my work on multiscale stochastic models, I use stochastic averaging and normalizing flows to learn reduced dynamics from an observed trajectory. I am also interested in Bayesian methods for inference in SDEs with unknown drift structure.

Reinforcement Learning and Bandits

I am interested in sequential decision-making under uncertainty, with a focus on reinforcement learning (RL) and contextual bandits. My interests include Bayesian and distributional approaches that model reward distributions and quantify uncertainty in the learning process.

A particular focus is offline learning, where decisions must be learned from previously collected data. I am interested in how uncertainty about outcomes and sensitivity to risk can be incorporated into policy learning and evaluation.

Gaussian Mixture Models and Video Denoising

I am interested in Gaussian mixture models (GMMs) as flexible probabilistic representations of complex data, and in their use for video denoising. This direction connects distribution learning with the recovery of clean signals from noisy observations.

My interests include learning mixture structure from noisy data and using it to guide reconstruction. In video denoising, I am interested in methods that preserve spatial detail and consistency across frames while accounting for uncertainty in the observations.
