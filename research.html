Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of stochastic processes, statistical inference, and machine learning. I am particularly interested in inference for stochastic differential equations, distribution learning and mixture models, and Bayesian and distributional reinforcement learning, with an emphasis on uncertainty quantification and probabilistic modeling.
